Household Readiness Survey
7 BENCHMARKS
1. Do you know India's Universal Emergency Number (112)?
2. Does your family have a pre-agreed disaster meeting point and communication plan?
3. Are critical family documents (Aadhaar, deeds, marksheets) in waterproof pouches or secure cloud storage?
4. Do you have a packed 72-hour survival kit (sealed water, ORS, dry food, torch, power bank)?
5. Have you identified the nearest designated Multipurpose Cyclone Shelter (MPCS) or high ground?
6. Do all adult family members know how to immediately shut off the electrical main breaker & LPG gas valve?
7. Do you have a stocked First Aid kit with essential chronic medications for elderly members?
